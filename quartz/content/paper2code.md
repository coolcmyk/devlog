>### definition:
paper2code is a cycle of implementations, where you start with a paper, and then implement it in code, and then test it, and then repeat.

>### why:
well if you wonder why am i doing all of this or just wondering what's my motivations:

- i believe that to be something or to get closer to a concept, you have to already act like it, doing the thing, see this [concept](https://strasberg.edu/about/what-is-method-acting/).
- i think it's important to always do a "low-level thinking" in our daily lives, just like muscles, you have a simple choice, whether you decided to use your brain or just lose its cognitive capabilities entirely.
